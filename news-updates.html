1. Trust officials addressed the ifs officers undergoing Skills Upgradation course and IFS probationers at IGNFA Dehradun.

IGNFA, Dehradun—August 17, 2026. The Trust addressed the officers attending the Skills Upgradation Course and the IFS probationers of the 2025 batch — on why the Trust was revived, what it has done in its first seven months, and what the senior members already working in SFDs for a long time and the youngest members of the Service can do to carry it forward.

2. Trust officials had an online interaction meeting with PCCF (HoFF), Odisha.

Pursuant to the decision of the GB, an online meeting was held with the PCCF (HoFF), Odisha. The Trust's objectives and assistance record were presented, along with the ways in which the State's field formations can partner with the Trust — in verifying case particulars promptly and in spreading the word among staff. Similar meetings with PCCF (HoFF) of other States/UTs follow in September and subsequently.

4. IFSBWT-Appeal letter to Hon'ble CM Gujarat.

The IFSBWT has written an appeal letter to the Honourable Chief Minister of Gujarat requesting the issuance of broad-based compensation orders for the forest field personnel who lay down their lives or get grievously injured in the line of duty.

The Trust is hopeful that the Government of Gujarat will take this request into consideration. We have provided an example of such a good order issued by the Government of Maharashtra.

The Trust proposes to write to the Chief Ministers of all those states where such general compensation orders are not available for forest field staff.

8. The Lesser Known Khaki — IGNFA Publication

The Lesser Known Khaki

"Jungle mein mor nacha, kisne dekha?" — When the peacock dances deep in the forest, who sees it?

In this striking artwork, Ms. Piyusha Pramod Jagtap, IFS (2014, Maharashtra), reveals the unseen world of the forester — and the quiet, demanding work behind the khaki we rarely notice.

9. Tragic case of late Shri Sohan Singh, Forest Guard, Uttarakhand

Shri Sohan Singh, a Forest Guard, Tons Forest Division, Uttarakhand, lost his life after falling in a deep gorge while battling a forest fire on June 17, 2026. Following a news clip shared by a member, the IFSBWT at once approached the concerned DFO for the details of the case in the prescribed format. The DFO responded with commendable promptness. The Trust, on June 21, 2026, approved financial assistance of ₹2 lakh to the mother of the deceased and the amount was credited to her account on June 22, 2026.

10. Case of Ms Rasmita Subuddhi, Forest Guard, Malkangiri Forest Division, Odisha

Ms Rasmita Subuddhi, Forest Guard, Malkangiri Forest Division, Odisha, was grievously injured on the night of June 12, 2026, while returning home from patrol duty. Acting on a message received in a WhatsApp group, the IFSBWT took cognisance of her condition, approved financial assistance of ₹2.5 lakh for her medical treatment on June 15, 2026, and released the amount on June 17, 2026.
