An amount of Rs. 1,35,000/- has been disbursed as a contribution to the bereaved family of late Shri Jitender Singh Shekhawat, Forest Guard, Rajasthan Forest Department

The IFSBWT held a press conference on 26.05.2026 at the Press Club of India, New Delhi.

Financial assistance of Rs. 3 lakh provided to the family of Shri Harikesh Singh Gurjar, Forest Guard, Morena District, Madhya Pradesh, who made a supreme sacrifice fighting the sand mining mafia on April 8, 2026.

Financial assistance of ₹2.0 lakh provided to the family of Thiru S. Radhakrishan, ACF, T N Forest Academy, Coimbatore on May 30, 2026, who was grievously injured in the line of duty on May 26, 2026 and was then under emergency treatment.

Financial assistance of ₹2.5 lakh was provided towards the emergency medical treatment of Ms Rasmita Subuddhi, FG, Malkangiri Division, Odisha, who was grievously injured in the line of duty on June 14 and required prolonged medical treatment and rehabilitation.

Financial assistance of ₹2 lakh was provided to the mother of the late Shri Sohan Singh, FG, Tonk Forest Division, Uttarakhand, who suffered a tragic accidental death while battling a forest fire on June 18, 2026.

₹3 lakh to the family of late Sh. Hari Om, Forester, Mahendergarh Forest Division, Haryana, who lost his life on July 13, 2026, when a truck hit his motorcycle from behind while going for an official meeting, disbursed on July 28, 2026.

₹2.00 lakh to the family of late Shri Vikas N. Desai, Range Forest Officer, Vyara (Tapi) Division, Gujarat, who died while chasing timber smugglers in a vehicle on July 20, 2026- disbursed on August 01, 2026.

₹1.50 lakh (first instalment) to the family of late Shri Lakhan Singh Maravi, Forest Fire Watcher, Kanha National Park, Madhya Pradesh, killed by a tiger while patrolling inside the park on June 14, 2026- disbursed on August 01, 2026.

₹1 lakh (first instalment) out of the financial assistance of ₹2 lakh approved, to the family members of Late Shri Shyamu Mahagu Sharnagat, FG, Navegaon-Nagzira Tiger Reserve, killed by a sloth bear August 9, 2026 while on patrol duty, disbursed on August 12, 2026.

₹1 lakh (first instalment) out of ₹2 lakh approved, to the family of Late Shri Naveen, FG, Palvancha Range, Bhadradri Kothagudem District, Telangana, who died of electrocution on the night of August 11, 2026 to apprehend poachers, disbursed on August 25, 2026.

Online Interaction meeting with the PCCF (HoFF), Odisha.

Pursuant to the decision of the GB, an online meeting was held with the PCCF (HoFF), Odisha. The Trust’s objectives and assistance record were presented, along with the ways in which the State’s field formations can partner with the Trust — in verifying case particulars promptly and in spreading the word among staff. Similar meetings with PCCF (HoFF) of other States/UTs follow in September and subsequently.
